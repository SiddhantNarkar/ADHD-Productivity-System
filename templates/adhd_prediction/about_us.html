Who We Are Know Our Story

We are a passionate and driven team of four emerging AI technologists - currently in our final year of Artificial Intelligence & Data Science at Dr. D.Y. Patil Institute of Technology, Pune. Our journey is fueled by curiosity, collaboration, and a shared vision to transform technology into meaningful solutions. With strong foundations in data-driven innovation, we aim to push boundaries by utilizing artificial intelligence to address real-world challenges that genuinely impact lives.

Our mission is to create purposeful and responsible technology that supports human well-being. Guided by core values such as empathy, trust, integrity, and innovation, we are currently developing AI for ADHD Productivity - a smart assistive system designed to help individuals better understand and manage ADHD through prediction, personalized support, and ongoing guidance. Our focus is on delivering intelligent solutions that enhance productivity, empower individuals with self-growth tools, and ensure that technology actively contributes to a healthier, more inclusive future. We believe that AI is not just about automation - it’s about elevating human potential, and our work reflects that commitment every step of the way.
