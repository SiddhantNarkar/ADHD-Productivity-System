Before You Begin the ADHD Assessment

This assessment is designed to better understand patterns related to attention, focus, impulsivity, and organization in everyday life. It is not a medical diagnosis, but a structured self-reflection tool that helps generate meaningful insights based on your responses.

Please read the following before starting:

  1. The assessment typically takes 10–15 minutes to complete.
  2. This assessment is completed in two parts:
    • Part 1: Multiple-choice questions based on daily experiences.
    • Part 2: A short, interactive gamified task.
    Completing both parts helps generate a more accurate and meaningful report.
  3. There are no right or wrong answers; respond honestly based on your usual experiences.
  4. Answer each question naturally and avoid overthinking your responses.
  5. If possible, take the assessment in a quiet, distraction-free space.
  6. Your responses are used only to generate insights and are not a clinical diagnosis.
